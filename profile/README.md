## What is LYNCH?

LYNCH is an AI-powered **website roasting** tool inspired by Lebedev Studio's "Business Lynch" and Reddit's *roast me* culture.

Drop a URL. In ~30 seconds you get:

- 🎯 An **overall score** with a savage one-liner summary
- 📸 A **screenshot with visual annotations** — every issue mapped to an exact spot
- 🔥 **Dynamic category scores** — tailored to what the site actually is
- 🛠️ **TOP-3 prioritized fixes** — what to act on first
- 🔗 A **shareable card** — perfect for Twitter/X

> *"Your CTA is hiding like a partisan in the woods."* — LYNCH AI

---

## How it works

LYNCH adapts its critique to the **type of site** being analyzed. A landing page gets judged on conversion and first impression. A blog gets judged on readability and content structure. An e-commerce store gets judged on trust and product presentation.

Categories are determined dynamically — you always get feedback that's actually relevant.

### Scoring

Each category is rated on a **star scale from 0.0 to 5.0** (half-stars included, e.g. 3.5 ★):

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ★★★★★ 4.5 – 5.0 | Excellent |
| ★★★★☆ 3.5 – 4.0 | Good, could be better |
| ★★★☆☆ 2.5 – 3.0 | Meh |
| ★★☆☆☆ 1.5 – 2.0 | Needs a rework |
| ★☆☆☆☆ 0.0 – 1.0 | Critically bad |
